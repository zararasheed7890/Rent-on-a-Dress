Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I reserve my dress?

We recommend submitting your availability request 1 to 3 weeks before your event, though we also accommodate last-minute next-day reservations on available pieces.

What if the dress doesn't fit as expected?

You can book a private fitting at our showroom prior to your event, or request a backup size (subject to availability) when submitting your reservation inquiry.

Do I need to dry clean the dress before returning it?

No! Please do not wash, iron, or dry clean the dress yourself. Every rental includes specialist couture cleaning at our partner atelier.

Can I rent a dress for more than 1 day?

Absolutely. Whether you need a dress for a single evening gala or a 3-day destination wedding weekend, simply select your start and return dates on the dress page.
